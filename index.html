    06

    Good questions

    The things worth knowing before we start. Still curious about something? Add it to your project note and I'll answer.

    How will I know where things stand?+

    Working files, written rationale, recorded walkthroughs, and a weekly written update: what's done, what's next, and what I need from you. Calls happen whenever they'd genuinely help.

    Will this fit our stack?+

    It's standards-based: DTCG tokens, Style Dictionary outputs to whatever platforms you ship, plain CSS variables if that's your stack. Your engineers see the pipeline before handoff, and the docs are written for them.

    What if my project doesn't fit one of these?+

    Get in touch anyway. Scoping is free, and if it isn't a fit I'll say so honestly rather than stretch one to fit.

    What do you need from me to start?+

    Access to the relevant tools, your brand assets, and a decision-maker who can respond within a day.

    How do revisions work?+

    Every project includes one structured revision round, with its scope written into the agreement so there are no surprises. What a round includes.

    Who does the work?+

    I do, on every project. One senior designer, no juniors and no outsourcing.

    Do you work with agencies?+

    Yes, white-label. The same projects, delivered under your name, and your client relationship stays yours. Just mention it when you reach out.
