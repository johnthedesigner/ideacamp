06

Good questions to ask

How do meetings work?

Live calls happen in set windows on Wednesdays and Fridays, and several offers need none at all. Everything else moves async.

What if my project doesn't fit an offer?

Send an intake anyway. Scoping is free, and if it isn't a fit I'll tell you honestly rather than stretch one to fit.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to the relevant tools, your brand assets, and one decision-maker who can respond within a day.

How do revisions work?

Each engagement includes one structured round of revisions, with its scope written into your agreement so there are no surprises.

Who actually does the work?

I do, on every project. One senior designer, no juniors and no outsourcing.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. White-label and overflow engagements run in these same shapes, and your client relationship stays yours. Just mention it in the intake.
