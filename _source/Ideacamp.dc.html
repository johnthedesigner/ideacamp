01

How the studio runs

Async-first

Work arrives as written docs, recorded walkthroughs, and working files. You review on your schedule. Progress never depends on getting people into a room.

Bounded sync

Live calls happen in scheduled windows, Wednesdays and Fridays. Some offers need zero meetings. Long stretches of deep work produce better design, faster.

Limited capacity

One or two active engagements at a time, never more. That is quality control. Your project gets a senior designer's full attention, not a slice of it.
