Ideacamp Design System · Style Preview

Structured skeleton, expressive skin.

A Swiss/editorial structural backbone carrying a Neo-Memphis expressive palette. Precise where it counts, playful on purpose. This tile proposes the visual direction for review before any tokens are generated.

01

Visual direction

Aesthetic references

Swiss / International Typographic (grid, numbered sections, typographic hierarchy, mono-flavored labels) as the structural backbone; Neo-Memphis / Post-Digital (bright categorical palette used as meaning, playful geometric shape accents, marquee) as the expressive skin; Editorial / Magazine (oversized Bricolage display, 400↔800 weight contrast) as the voice.

Color commitment — Level 3, Full Palette

Primary C 0.250; expressive accents C 0.15–0.22; working surfaces held near-neutral (C 0.003–0.02) but sharing one violet hue temperature (H 285–300°). The categorical palette is load-bearing brand (color-as-meaning), not decoration — the definition of Level 3. Not Level 4 (surfaces stay quiet so brights read as deliberate); not Level 2 (color is dominant to the identity).

Typographic character

Three families, distinct registers. Bricolage Grotesque display/headings (700–800, 22→~100px, tracking −0.02 to −0.035em). Geist body/UI (400–600, 15–19px, lh 1.55–1.6). Nunito micro-labels/eyebrows (500–700, 11.5–13px, uppercase, +0.10–0.12em) for rounded warmth at small sizes. Range 11.5px→~100px; weight 400→800.

Shape — friendly-to-rounded, tiered

A deliberate radius hierarchy, never uniform: chips/badges 8px · buttons & inputs 12px · cards 18px · large cards/modals 22px · avatars/pills full. Anchored by the recent move from full-radius pills to 12px rounded-rectangle buttons.

What this is NOT

  • Not generic SaaS: no #FAFAFA gray surfaces, no medium 220–240° blue (ours is H267° electric violet-blue), no uniform 6–8px radius, no pure-black shadows.
  • Not enterprise-minimal: the categorical palette and shape accents are brand, not strippable chrome.
  • Not bubbly-pill or soft-UI: tiered rounded rectangles (not full pills), flat surfaces with restrained elevation, no neumorphism or glassmorphism.

07

Reviewer notes

Decisions that needed judgment and warrant your explicit sign-off before token generation:

  • Pure-white → tinted surfaces. The current site uses #FFFFFF as the paper (an absolute ban). Proposed: page surface #F7F6FB, cards #FDFCFF, overlays #FEFDFF — all sharing the violet temperature. This is a subtle but real shift from today's pure white. Confirm the off-white page background is acceptable.
  • Secondary = pink (#FF4D8D). The brand has no single historical "secondary" — it has a categorical set. Pink is nominated because it contrasts strongly with the blue primary and avoids colliding with any semantic hue. Alternative: lilac (#9B6DFF). Confirm pink as the semantic secondary, or pick another.
  • Semantic vs. categorical split. Blue→primary, pink→secondary, mint→success, yellow→warning, coral→error; the full six brights remain a documented categorical accent set for sectioning/data. Note pink (secondary, H3°) and coral (error, H33°) are adjacent warm hues — distinguishable but worth a glance. Confirm the mapping, especially pink-secondary vs coral-error proximity.
  • Dark-ink-on-bright signature. White text fails AA on mint (2.15:1), coral (3.10:1), and pink (3.14:1), so all bright fills use ink text. This is consistent with the site's coral button and yellow badge. If a solid error/secondary button must have white text, its base color would need darkening in Step 4. Confirm dark-on-bright is the intended look.
  • Three type families. Bricolage + Geist + Nunito each hold a distinct register (display / body / micro-label). Confirm Nunito earns its place for labels, vs. folding labels into Geist.
  • Radius hierarchy. 8 / 12 / 18 / 22 + full. Buttons at 12px reflect the recent pill→rectangle change. Confirm the card radius (18–22px) reads as intended, not too soft.
  • Contrast status: every text and UI pairing above passes WCAG 2.2 AA as labeled. Container-text and muted-text pairings verified ≥ 4.5:1. No failing pair is shipped in this tile.

Note on method: OKLCH values and contrast ratios here were computed locally for preview. Step 4 will regenerate the full primitive ramps using the Sistema palette generator and re-verify every on-* pairing through the Sistema contrast API, per the brief.
